## 💡 What is FixWise AI?

When a device breaks, most people face the same question:

> **"Should I repair it or replace it?"**

The problem is that users often don't know:

- What exactly is wrong with the device
- Whether the issue is serious
- How repairable the device actually is
- How much the repair may cost
- Whether repairing it makes financial sense
- Whether replacing it would be a better option
- Where to find a suitable repairer
- How to track the repair after handing over the device

**FixWise AI brings these decisions into one platform.**

Users can upload a device image, describe the problem, receive an AI-assisted diagnosis, compare repair and replacement options, evaluate repairability, explore repairers and quotes, and track the repair journey.

                 ✨ Why FixWise?

FixWise isn't simply an image-diagnosis chatbot.

It is designed as a complete repair decision platform.

🔍 Diagnose

Use AI-assisted visual analysis to understand potential device issues.

📊 Evaluate

Estimate repairability, safety risk, expected repair cost and device condition.

⚖️ Compare

Compare the economics of repairing the existing device versus replacing it.

🛠️ Find Repairers

Explore suitable repair options and repair providers.

💰 Compare Quotes

Evaluate available repair quotes before making a decision.

📍 Track Repairs

Keep track of an ongoing repair instead of losing visibility after handing over the device.

🪪 Repair Passports

Maintain a structured history of a device's repair journey.
